Ninety seconds, and nothing to buy
This is the last one we will send about your reading. There is nothing to buy in this email — no button, no offer, nothing at the bottom.

You answered twenty-one honest questions and we never gave you anything for it. So here is the first ritual from the plan, written out in full. It is yours whether or not you ever open the rest.
Tonight, before bed
Ninety seconds, in three parts
0:30
Hold something with weight
A stone, your keys, a cup of tea still warm. Rest it in your open palm and let your hand feel how heavy it is. Nothing else.
0:30
Let the breath out longer than it came in
In for four, out for six. Four times through. If you lose count, start again — losing count is not failing.
0:30
Say one finished thing out loud
One thing you completed today, however small. Out loud, in the room, not in your head. The saying is the part that works.
The third part is the one people skip and the one that matters. A mind that has been counting what is unfinished all day needs to hear, in your own voice, that something got finished.
